      🌌 What is The Gravity Grid?

      The Gravity Grid is a large-scale gravity-driven strategic board game inspired by Tic-Tac-Toe and Connect Four, engineered for 2 to 8 players across configurable grids from 10×10 up to 25×25. Unlike free-placement games, gravity dictates every move: pieces always drop to the lowest unoccupied cell in the selected column.

      📏 Core Rules & Win Conditions

      • Select any non-full column (or press number/arrow keys + Enter) to drop your insignia.
      • Pieces fall under gravity to the lowest open row in that column.
      • Win by aligning Connect 4, 5, 6, or custom N pieces along enabled directions: Horizontal, Vertical, Diagonal, or Anti-Diagonal.
      • In Team Mode, teammates share victory when any teammate completes a valid line—or build cross-player combos depending on turn strategy.

      🕹️ Game Modes Explained

      • Classic: Standard gravity strategy until a player connects N or the board fills.
      • Sudden Death: High-stakes blitz clock—running out of time eliminates you or hands victory to the opponent.
      • Timed: Strict per-turn countdown and overall match timer pressure.
      • Endless: Every line of N awards points and clears or scores continuously until the grid is completely full; highest score wins!
      • Tournament: Automated multi-match round-robin standings across participants.
      • Chaos Mode: Periodic gravity anomalies! Columns can shuffle, bottom rows can compress, or wildcard drops trigger every few turns.

      🤖 AI Difficulty Levels (1–7)

      • Level 1 — Random: Chooses valid columns unpredictably.
      • Level 2 — Basic Strategy: Takes immediate wins and favors center control.
      • Level 3 — Offensive: Aggressively builds multi-threat lines and forks.
      • Level 4 — Defensive: Prioritizes blocking opponent 2-way traps and imminent threats.
      • Level 5 — Minimax: Alpha-beta pruned lookahead search evaluating future replies.
      • Level 6 — Advanced Heuristic: Deep positional evaluation of open windows, parity, and trap setups.
      • Level 7 — Adaptive Learning: Tracks your preferred columns and tactical habits across the session to counter your patterns!

      🏆 Scoring, XP & Achievements

      Every move, victory, tournament win, and daily challenge earns XP. Level up to climb ranks from Gravity Initiate to Cosmic Grandmaster, unlock exclusive player titles, and collect achievements like First Victory, Fast Thinker, Theme Explorer, and Gravity Scholar.
